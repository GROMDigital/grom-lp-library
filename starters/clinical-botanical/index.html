Good to know

A few honest answers

Why a £40 deposit?

It secures your appointment time and comes straight off the £160 price, so you're never paying extra, just paying a little in advance.

Can I cancel or reschedule?

Yes. Give us 24 hours notice and we'll happily move your session to a time that suits you better, free of charge, and your deposit moves with it. Inside 24 hours, or if you don't attend, the deposit can't be refunded.

Will I be red or need downtime?

Some light redness is common for a few hours afterwards, similar to mild sun exposure. Most people return to their normal routine the same day.

How many sessions will I need?

One session is designed to stand complete on its own. Your practitioner may suggest a short course for particular goals, but that choice is always yours.

Will this suit my skin?

This treatment is designed to suit most skin types. Your practitioner will talk through your skin and history before starting, so you know exactly what to expect.
