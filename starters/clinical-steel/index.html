Common questions

Answered plainly

What does the £75 session include?

The £75 session is a personalised, one to one appointment. You talk through your goals and the areas you would like to change, have a body contouring treatment, and get honest guidance on what would suit you next. It is secured with a £25 deposit when you book, which comes off the price.

Is there a deposit, and what if I need to change my appointment?

The session is £75. To secure your appointment we take a £25 deposit when you book, and it comes off the price. To move your appointment, we ask for 48 hours notice.

Is it a treatment or a consultation?

It is both. Your £75 session includes a body contouring treatment on the area you would like to work on, and it is also when we look at your goals and advise what would suit you next. If a treatment course is right for you, we explain the options and pricing clearly, with no pressure to decide on the day.

Where is the clinic, and what are the opening hours?

Verano Body Clinic is at 22 Prospect Street, Harrogate, HG1 1AA. We are open Tuesday to Thursday 9:30am to 8pm, Friday 9:30am to 6:30pm, and Saturday 9:30am to 5pm. The clinic is closed on Sunday and Monday.

How do I know if body contouring may be suitable for me?

The best way to know is to be assessed properly during your session. We will understand your goals, look at what may be suitable, and explain the recommended next steps. Suitability is decided in person, not promised beforehand.
