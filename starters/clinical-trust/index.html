What the consultation includes

A careful hour before any decision is made

The consultation is where the real work happens. You sit with Dr Warner, you are properly assessed, and you leave understanding your options clearly, whether or not you decide to go ahead.

01

Facial assessment

A structured read of your face at rest and in movement, mapping proportion, symmetry and the areas you are most conscious of.

02

Medical history

A full review of your health, medications and any past treatments, so every recommendation is made on medical ground, not on a template.

03

Honest suitability advice

A candid view on whether a treatment is right for you at all. If it is not the right time, or not right for you, Dr Warner will tell you plainly.

04

A considered plan

If you choose to proceed, you leave with a written plan set to your priorities and paced sensibly, never rushed into more than you asked for.

05

Time to think

No same-day pressure. You are welcome to take your plan home, sit with it, and book only when you feel genuinely ready.

Complimentary, always

The consultation is free and carries no obligation. It exists so you can make a calm, well-informed choice.
