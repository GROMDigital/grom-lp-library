Common questions

Answered plainly

What does the complimentary consultation cover?

Your consultation is a calm, one to one appointment with a qualified clinician. You talk through your skin concerns and goals, have your skin assessed properly, and get honest, evidence-led guidance on what would suit you next. It is complimentary and secures your time when you book.

Is it really free, or is there a deposit?

It is genuinely complimentary. There is no deposit and no card needed to book, and there is no obligation to go ahead with anything afterwards. To move your appointment, we simply ask for 48 hours notice.

Do I need to commit to a treatment?

No. The consultation is an assessment, not a sales appointment. If a treatment plan is right for you, we explain the options and what to expect clearly, with no pressure to decide on the day. Many people take the guidance home and think it over first.

Where is the clinic, and what are the opening hours?

Northcott Skin & Laser is at Suite 4, 12 Victoria Avenue, Chatswood NSW 2067. We are open Tuesday to Friday 9:00am to 6:00pm, and Saturday 9:00am to 3:00pm. The clinic is closed on Sunday and Monday.

How do I know if a treatment may suit my skin?

The best way to know is to be assessed properly during your consultation. We will understand your concerns, review your skin, and explain what may be suitable and the recommended next steps. Suitability is decided in person, not promised beforehand.
