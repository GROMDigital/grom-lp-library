Good Questions

Everything you might be wondering

These are the questions we hear most before a first visit, answered plainly.

Still deciding? Book a consultation
What does the consultation cost, and what is included?

The Skin Health Consultation is $189 and runs about sixty minutes. It includes a full skin assessment, a written plan, and a gentle resurfacing facial. Any treatment beyond that is quoted clearly before you commit.

How long should I set aside?

Allow an hour. We would rather take our time than move you through quickly, so we never double-book a consultation slot.

Where are you, and is there parking?

We are on St Georges Road in Fitzroy North, a short walk from the tram. There is unmetered street parking on the surrounding streets and a stop almost at the door.

My skin is sensitive. Is a first visit still suitable?

Yes. The first visit is an assessment, not an aggressive treatment. We choose gentle options and always patch-test where a product or peel is new to you. Individual suitability is confirmed by your clinician.

Who will I see on the day?

A registered nurse or dermal clinician, every time. You will meet the person guiding your plan at the consultation itself, not just at treatment.
