Your questions, answered

What exactly do I get for $69?

Your first visit covers a skin and suitability check, a patch test, and one treatment area of your choice. A $29 deposit holds the booking and comes off the $69 on the day, so you pay $40 in-studio.

How long will I be there?

Most first sessions take around twenty minutes once you include the skin check and patch test. Single-area treatments themselves are usually just a few minutes.

Where are you and is parking easy?

We are on Glebe Road in Merewether, minutes from the beach. There is free street parking nearby and we are on the main bus route.

Is laser right for my skin?

That is exactly what the first check is for. Your therapist reviews your skin and history and only proceeds if it is suitable for you. Suitability and comfort vary from person to person, which is why we never skip this step.

Who will treat me?

One of our qualified laser therapists, every time. They will walk you through each step and you are welcome to ask questions at any point during your session.
