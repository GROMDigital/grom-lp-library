What does the $89 signature session include?

Your $89 session is a full signature skin treatment tailored to your skin on the day, plus a warm one to one consultation about your goals. You leave with honest guidance on what would suit your skin next. It is normally $140.

Is there a deposit, and what if I need to reschedule?

A $25 deposit secures your appointment when you book, and it comes off your $89 session. To move your appointment, we simply ask for 48 hours notice.

Is it a treatment or a consultation?

It is both. The signature session includes a real skin treatment and a proper conversation about your skin. If an ongoing plan would suit you, we explain the options and the pricing clearly, with no pressure to decide on the day.

How do I know what would suit my skin?

The best way to know is to be assessed in person during your session. We look at your skin, listen to your goals, and give you a straight, honest read. Suitability is assessed in person, never promised beforehand.

Where is the studio, and what are the hours?

Sunna Skin Studio is at Shop 3, 41 The Corso, Manly NSW 2095. We are open Tuesday to Friday 9:30am to 7pm and Saturday 9:00am to 4pm, closed Sunday and Monday.
